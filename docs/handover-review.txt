execution at activation can potentially place every possible resulting public

and safe-set construction use established ideas; their application to this known
small economic graph is not a new general shielding algorithm.

## Results worth reporting and hostile interpretation

For the four same-case cap-reference labels, the one-round competitive cap
certifies the relaxed 90%, 95%, 99% and 100% targets in respectively 8, 5, 4 and
4 of the eight configurations. It certifies all eight near-Nash-anchor labels.
Every failed target in this particular matrix has every state certified unsafe;
there are no observed ambiguous failures or nonempty-safe-set/no-prefix-cap
cases. The synthetic tests retain those logical distinctions so this observed
coincidence does not become a general theorem.

The 100% label is not an exact unrelaxed guarantee. For example, the base 15-grid
requested floor is 72.1190404181283, the actual threshold is 72.11903969693789,
and the post-cap guard interval is [72.11904040370447,72.1190404181283]. Report
all three quantities when a precision-sensitive statement depends on equality.

All-anchor success does not mean all cases benefit from initialization. The
affine-equivalent scale/lower-quality pair has the same asymptotic worst floor
from every original state, so initialization adds no long-run improvement there.
In the other six configurations it raises the worst guard tail available from
all eight saved unprotected-training endpoints to the anchor floor. For base 15,
that moves the attained worst tail from 68.477352 to 72.119040. For base 31 it
moves 67.271289 to 68.960917, still below the permanent-cap floor 72.362996.
These are deterministic counterfactual guarantees, not new empirical learning
effects or behavioral success rates.

This is a meaningful, verified defense consequence of the prior basin analysis.
It is also small-model exhaustive calculation with strong knowledge assumptions.
Established safe-region reasoning and prefix enumeration do not become a novel
shielding method. A reasonable provisional execution assessment is about 4/5;
incremental originality remains around 3/5, not a forecast of official scoring.
Clarity depends on the forthcoming presentation retaining the threshold, scope

coarser cases fails at 31 prices, while the initializer still improves its basin.
An independently reviewed continuous-price or mesh-limit argument could separate
a useful robust handover principle from a quantization-specific safe basin.
Any exact-Nash impossibility theorem must not be substituted for the nearest-grid
cap or its relaxed targets without a quantified gap. New structural-order or
continuous proofs remain outside this passed numerical certificate until reviewed.

A discounted unilateral best-response audit is still necessary before claiming
that a coordinated policy is credible self-enforcing collusion. It is not needed
for the present all-policy defense statement. If pursued, predeclare a complete
off-path opponent policy and discount, check all states and each seller separately,
and certify Bellman residual/error bounds rather than searching afterward for a
convenient stable-looking loop. These questions should decide the next goal;
another attractive replay alone will not resolve them.

## Separately reviewed next-goal mathematics, not initializer evidence

The candidate order-envelope induction is valid: if nonempty allowed sets have a
nondecreasing maximum U, the maximum-permitted trajectory from the same initial
pair dominates every feasible trajectory coordinatewise at every time. Decreasing
consumer surplus then makes that path simultaneously worst at every future round
and for every finite-horizon sum, even for randomized or history-dependent rivals.
Interval support is unnecessary. Iteration of finite monotone U reaches a fixed
point from each scalar start; the two-shop envelope eventually holds the two
resulting fixed points or swaps them. Thus an off-diagonal two-cycle can be the
start-specific optimum even though the prior global witnesses were stationary.

The reviewer separately checked the continuous candidate's algebra: Lambert-W
best response and upper-root expression, derivative sign d*z-t, the conservative
small-tolerance condition, the explicit nonmonotonic counterexample, best-response
contraction, positive diagonal-profit derivative, second-derivative bound, explicit
eta/q construction and welfare-loss expression. They are valid under the stated
interior-best-response and compact-domain assumptions. The monotonicity
counterexample correctly prevents treating U as universally increasing.

The most consequential continuous statement is that every initial public pair
admits a permitted best-response approach followed by a profitable above-Nash
stationary tail. It rules out exact continuous-Nash welfare protection by finite
initialization in that model. It does not alone defeat a specific relaxed finite-
grid reference: an explicit certified loss must exceed the actual benchmark gap.
No new scenario evaluation or claim that these candidates are novel has been
performed in this review. These are prospective mathematical checks, separate
from the frozen 40-label numerical certificate and pending new-goal evidence.

The root's subsequent derivative identity also checks: for upper-root normalized
profit a=w-t, the numerator of u_w minus f_x equals (t/w)*(1-a/u)>0, while f_x<0.
Therefore u_w<1; under the separately established positive-derivative condition,
0<u_w<1. The clipped continuous upper map is then rho-contractive, yielding one
above-Nash fixed point and the same worst long-run welfare from every initial
state by the order envelope. This is a conditional candidate theorem, not an
additional empirical initializer result. A proposed mesh bound requires uniform
allowed width and root sensitivity across the expanded tolerance range. It may
show convergence of basin welfare values without eliminating multiple basins on
every finite grid. For arbitrary permitted policies one must bound positive
price excess above the continuous fixed point; absolute-distance contraction
applies to the top-choice path, not to sellers deliberately quoting low prices.
