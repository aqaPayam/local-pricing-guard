## What the guarantees mean

Under the validated A<q_star and inherited monotonicity/contraction assumptions, the maximum-allowed-price policy attains the smallest customer reward at every phase. Its descending cycle rewards give both the long-run average floor G_K and a floor G_K for EVERY average measured from immediate cap activation. The worst individual round is separately saved and can lie below G_K. An arbitrary initial phase has only the long-run conclusion unless its preceding partial cycle is separately bounded.

If the old target is already at or below C_star, zero recurring activations suffice for the long-run target. Doing nothing has zero startup caps; one real immediate cap has one startup cap and additionally gives the stated finite-activation-average floor. These policies are not merged. Infeasibility or censoring, if encountered, applies only to the declared family/search and cannot rule out other persistent controllers.

The 64 explicit reward terms and a64 are followed by an analytic geometric-tail enclosure, not invented simulated rewards. Each uncertain rival interval is mapped using two separately signed scalar endpoint roots. Saved seller profits describe the CS-worst path; they are not profit guarantees, joint-profit optima or repeated-game incentive tests.
