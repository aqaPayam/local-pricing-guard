In the loop, One initial limit always uses the guard. Repeated limits uses the same guard, then a price limit at step 4. Both keep the full previous price pair.

Guard: each shop may choose prices whose modeled immediate profit is close to its best against the rival’s previous price. Price limit: replaces that guard for the round and allows prices at or below the fixed ceiling.

Matching the clock does not make joint learning stationary or prove convergence. The saved replay below freezes both programs.

What is controlled, and what is not?

Both arms use the same five clock labels, dense allocated Q capacity, zero initialization, paired random streams and two-million-round budget. Ongoing legal constraints change experiences during learning and during the displayed continuation. This comparison does not isolate policy formation from continued enforcement.

The seed identities are intentionally reused from earlier work. This is a staged follow-up in one saved finite model, not a new independent market cohort, equilibrium test or evidence that collusion was prevented. No confidence interval is planned.
