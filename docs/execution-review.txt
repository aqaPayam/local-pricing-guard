# Independent methods and clarity review: robustness extension

This is an AI-agent methods review, not an official score or human validation.
The reviewer owns a separate arithmetic/transition auditor and does not author
the experiment, its analysis, the report or the interface. Agreement is not a
requirement to award a high score. Status: prospective review and full numerical

## What the existing work establishes

The original CREDIT_RESULTS, final report and SPRINT_FINAL_GRADING support a
bounded measurement result: changing the action interface can make retained
proposals perfect without widening permitted prices or improving protected
economic outcomes. Longer deployment materially revised the apparent advantage
of a simple cap. Actual learners, matched seeds, release and independent
evaluation replay distinguish this from merely animating programmed behavior.
Nevertheless, a singleton permitted set in one fixed calibration makes the
economic equality structurally unsurprising. Established shielding and
feasible-action Q-learning limit innovation. More checks of the same arithmetic
would not alone improve the research contribution.

## Prospective changes requested before outcome generation

1. Change actual economics, using economy-specific competitive and joint-profit
   benchmarks and price grids. Different random seeds alone do not test economic
   robustness. The proposed four calibrations change product substitution,
   product attractiveness relative to the outside option, and cost.
2. Separate price-grid density and economically scaled profit-loss tolerance.
   Enumerate complete off-path guard geometry, then report on-path permitted
   count, price span and singleton frequency without selecting favorable cells.
3. Fork each saved policy into a 2-by-2 of exploration off/on and Q-updates
   off/on, both protected and released. These controls separate changes caused
   by continued sampling from changes requiring updating at deployment.
4. Use 20,000 evaluation rounds with a predeclared final-5,000 window. A
   3,000-round-only endpoint would repeat the transient-comparison weakness.
   Neither window proves convergence, equilibrium or meaningful discretion.
5. Retain every declared seed and arm, archive hashes before training, preserve
   previous results, and independently replay all available evaluation traces.
   Report any incomplete run or failure rather than silently selecting results.

The proposed budget is eight learned configuration cells, eight seeded runs per
cell, four matched learner/controller arms and three million training rounds
per arm. The independent unit for each within-cell contrast is the seed;
branches, rounds, arms and late-window rows do not increase n. The tolerance
anchor should recover exactly .1 in the original economy. Raw welfare levels
must remain separated across differently scaled economies; normalized paired
effects can supplement them. A lower product quality strengthens, rather than
weakens, the outside option; names must describe that change correctly.

## Interpretation restrictions

These are still symmetric deterministic two-seller logit models, tabular agents,
fixed learning hyperparameters and finite training/evaluation horizons. The
follow-up is designed after earlier findings and is not an independent
preregistration of the original claim. A prospectively frozen follow-up is
valuable but does not remove that dependency. All conclusions need their cell,
protection state and reporting window.

Proposal retention measures interface bookkeeping. Feasible count and span
measure modeled permission; they do not establish valuable business freedom.
Marginal execution entropy measures realized variation. Entropy conditioned on
the previous rival index is a more specific descriptive diagnostic, not full
state-conditional strategic freedom. High prices are harmful pricing under the
stated screen, not a legal collusion certificate or evidence of intent.

The independent auditor must recompute economics, continuous/grid benchmarks,
guard maps, feasibility, next-state aware bootstrap support, PRNG draws,
selection, Q updates, final checkpoints, raw/late-window outcomes, conditional
counts, aggregation and seeded uncertainty without importing the experiment
engine. Corrupted action, Q and aggregate examples must be rejected. Full
training replay must not be claimed unless it is actually performed.

A post-freeze analytic qualification was identified before interpreting the
changed-economy outcomes: scale0.5 and lower-quality share the same
dimensionless demand shape. With x=(p-cost)/mu and
theta=(quality-cost-outsideQuality)/mu, demand depends only on x and theta;
profits and consumer surplus scale by buyers*mu. The four calibrations cover
three theta levels: 4, 2, 2 and 5.6. Consequently the two theta=2 cells provide
an affine-unit/normalization crosscheck rather than independent economic
generalization. All frozen cells remain in the run. This is disclosed as an
analytic qualification, not retroactively added as a prespecified outcome.
Ideal real-arithmetic geometry invariance does not guarantee identical finite
floating-point Q-learning trajectories when greedy values are nearly tied.

## Completed numerical audit and accepted results

The substantive run completed in 578.7136 seconds without failed arms: 256
training labels/768 million rounds, followed by 2,048 evaluation branches and
40.96 million saved evaluation transitions. The new independently authored
auditor passed at 2026-10-25T19:28:23.109Z, with 294,735,229 checks, maximum
arithmetic discrepancy 4.440892098500626e-16 and zero greedy-tie exceptions.
The check count includes deliberate rejection tests; it is not an independent
sample size or a claim about external validity.

The audit covers all 36 geometry cells and nine affine-equivalent geometry
pairs; every saved evaluation action, PRNG draw, execution, Q update, final
checkpoint, diagnostic sample and bin; both reporting windows; all 4,096
dependent analysis views, 512 summary groups and 1,280 paired contrasts. It
independently regenerates the 10,000-resample whole-seed percentile intervals.
In-memory corrupted execution, terminal Q and aggregate examples were rejected.
Frozen live/snapshot sources and earlier checkpoint hashes match. No original
study outcome file was rewritten. **The 768 million training transitions were
not independently replayed.** Fast/general and late-clock parity tests provide
implementation evidence for training, not a substitute claim of full replay.

The extension materially improves the scope and controls of the bounded
question. In protected, updating, exploration-.01 full-20k evaluation, four
configurations have singleton visited-state support; the four
remaining configurations have multiple permitted actions, including the
affine-equivalent calibration pair. The singleton premise is conditional, not
a general fact about the guard. Nevertheless aware retention is 100% in all
cells: it cannot distinguish those support geometries. In the 31-price base
cell aware permitted count is about 3.018 and CS 69.004, versus cap CS 72.365;
cap profit A is about 21.910 versus aware 23.397. Thus better consumer benefit
and better seller profit remain different objectives.

The 2-by-2 supplies a concrete, limited mechanism check. For every newly trained
cap configuration, turning Q updates on/off leaves CS unchanged at a fixed
exploration rate. In the 31-price aware-guard illustration, exploration alone
raises CS by .031351, paired interval [.022249, .045435]; adding updates at
exploration .01 adds .012173 [.006905, .016173]. These small changes belong to
the new trained cohorts. They do not identify why the original unconstrained-
checkpoint cap comparison had a larger transient. Illustrations selected after
examining the complete table are explanatory, not fresh confirmatory tests.

Remaining execution limits are substantive: three dimensionless shapes in the
same demand family, eight seed units per cell, fixed learning hyperparameters,
different state coverage as grids grow under equal round budgets, no convergence
or equilibrium certificate, no full independent training replay, no LLM or
semantic mechanism, and no human comprehension validation. Duplicate-equivalent
calibrations and repeated no-control/cap arms across tolerance cells do not add
independent economic regimes. The main scientific novelty remains incremental.
