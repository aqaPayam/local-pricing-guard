## Meaningful addition and remaining limits

The useful result is a model-specific characterization of the cap/guard timing
family and a quantified integer-period penalty. It is stronger than showing
the behavior of a programmed seller: the conditional proof covers every legal
seller policy, and a feasible causal adverse price path establishes necessity.
The exact funded calendar is an implemented witness, not empirical discovery
of how strategic learners will behave.

Adjacent threshold mixing, Jensen/renewal optimization and pathwise budget
funding have close prior literature already credited in the frozen novelty
note. Generic algorithm or first-priority claims remain unjustified. The
pricing reduction, preservation of the original physical cap/absolute targets,
all-prefix realization and numerical certificate are incremental application
contributions. They do not establish incentive compatibility, learner adaptation,
intent, collusion detection, robust regulation under unknown economics or
real-market welfare effects. Saved old phase-Q policies cannot be transferred
to this different calendar state.

The objective counts cap-rule activations, including caps that change no quote.
It is not a monetary implementation-cost, changed-price or autonomy metric.
All-prefix safety starts with the actual first cap; it is not a sliding-window
or every-round guarantee. The deterministic conservative calendar does not
spend additional cooperative reward on longer gaps. Other history-adaptive
controllers can behave differently on cooperative paths while sharing the same
restricted worst-path optimum. No claim of uniquely optimal realized behavior
follows from the minimax result.
