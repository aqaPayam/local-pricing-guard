## Verdict

This is a meaningful, bounded follow-up to the welfare experiments. A welfare
floor and a profitable unilateral response answer different questions. The new
study measures the latter against the actual frozen learners, instead of
programming a response and merely showing its behavior. The response is checked
against a global, all-action upper certificate, so its near-optimality is not
inferred from a favorable trajectory alone.

The result is incremental research, not a new optimal-control algorithm, proof of
collusion or general equilibrium test. Frozen-opponent MDP deviations already
appear in the close Lopes de Moraes and De Losso prior. The addition is the
phase/history-correct guarded application, connection to the earlier welfare
question, and independently reproducible achieved-value/upper-gap evidence.
