What does this tell us?

At discount 19/20, in every tested recurring setting, each shop can gain a little by departing from the highest-price script. A simple deviation in the last guard round already shows why: the next cap resets history, so it removes the future effect of that choice. The exact calculation measures the gain.

This tests a fixed, public opponent. It does not show learned collusion, a stable equilibrium, or how real businesses or people behave. A zero gain in one setting does not establish a general equilibrium.

Inspect every recorded phase & both discounts

The game uses a discount of 19/20. The research also keeps discount zero: the first cap can then have zero gain even when a later guard phase has a profitable deviation. No zero result is hidden.
