# Profit challenge: developer verification

This is a local playable interface against a specified public deterministic
price script. Optional hints read stored, independently audited policy actions.
These checks did not run an LLM or other model as a game player, call an
experimental seller/model API, train agents or collect human participant data.
Codex assisted implementation and AI-agent review; that assistance is separate
from the experimental decision model.

## Independent controller review and synthetic tests

Source inspection identified two substantive failure-handling gaps in the new
challenge wrapper. The root agent corrected both before final checks: all-state
stored policies are validated for integer, permitted choices at construction,
and a final score exceeding its certificate is rejected before mutating prices,
history, ledger or discount state. Its synthetic seller-B policy fixture was
also corrected to be legal at every state, including states off the example path.

The four dedicated challenge-core tests pass alongside the 15 frozen primary
tests. These cover asymmetric seller reward coordinates, first-cap execution,
lagged public state, exact complete-block scoring, undo, illegal choices, stored
strategy validation, K = 1 and atomic rejection. Numerical experiment source and
results were not changed by these interface fixes.

## Actual-data controller and DOM checks

`node .local/incentive-display-smoke.mjs` passed every **48 playable variants**:
eight saved finite games, three period slots and two focal shops, all with the
exact discount 19/20. The underlying research retains both discounts and all
96 comparison labels; the challenge deliberately selects only the primary
discount for manual play.

For all 48 variants the harness checked the initial zero-reward state, current
support against the rival's previous executed price, the public opponent's quote
against the focal shop's previous executed price, original seller-A/B table
coordinates, first actual cap, per-round outcomes and nonnegative customer-floor
buffer. Reading a hint left all state unchanged. Following hints for a complete
block matched the independently audited exact optimum; copying each highest
permitted quote matched the exact saved baseline. All 48 variants also passed
undo/reset and a changed-first-action path followed by hints from its resulting
actual public states. Rejected post-completion actions did not advance the game.

There were 1,098 deterministic controller executions, including undo/replay and
alternative paths. These are automated software checks, not behavioral trials
or new experimental observations. The 48 optimum comparisons retained 34
strictly positive and 14 zero complete-block gains. They are not independent
markets or statistical replications. A separate saved gamma-zero example checked
that a zero activation gain coexists with a later profitable recorded phase.

The same harness ran the actual frontend with a lightweight DOM adapter through
all 48 configurations and 398 deterministic executions, including undo/replay.
It checked that hints and high/low suggestions fill a choice without executing,
unfinished scores are not labeled shortfalls, completion uses matching complete
blocks, zero findings remain visible, both-discount phase tables remain present,
and restart restores blank earned outcomes. The saved local JSON blob was
inspected under an intercepted download API and explicitly labels its record
as manual local play rather than participant data. This does not verify a real
browser download.

Malformed actions, missing/duplicate policy layers, invalid identities/discounts,
inconsistent exact comparison values, missing audit approval, failed loading and
unavailable evidence were rejected. A malformed too-low optimal score was also
tested to reject atomically. A small positive amount used scientific notation
rather than ordinary displayed zero. Simulated chart widths 390/320 and resize
to a wide view exercised the corresponding SVG branches; these checks are not
browser layout or mobile-device validation.
