# Prospective matched-clock analysis contract

Status: REVIEWED PROSPECTIVE ANALYSIS CONTRACT, Goal10, outside closed V11.
Root and independent source review accept these analysis choices before any
new matched-study outcome. This document alone authorizes no real training,
Q decoding, endpoint calculation or outcome export. The protocol's maximal
artificial benchmark, source/input/runtime freeze, administrative closure and
separate root numerical GO remain required before main execution; authoritative
inventory and endpoint-specific replay acceptance precede interpretation.

## Planned inventory and primary endpoint

Retain two original-tolerance `base-g15-t1` arms with the same five-phase dense
architecture, zero initialization and ordered seeds 907001..907012: 24 runs,
two checkpoints each. Use stable new IDs such as
`base-g15-t1--matched5-<startup|recurring4>--zero--s<seed>`; no old compact
run/checkpoint is overwritten or substituted. Seed identities are intentionally
reused, not newly independent of the prior study.

Primary `pairedRows[12]` records the exact saved-weight final actual-terminal
frozen-cycle customer-benefit difference, recurring minus startup, for each
ordered whole-market seed. Each row carries both run IDs, completion and replay
status, source/checkpoint/graph identities, actual NEXT phase/history, exact
cycle values, signed exact difference and a separate approximate display.
Zero/negative differences remain visible. Missing performance is null with a
reason, never zero. Sellers, rounds, phases and checkpoints are not sample units.

The planned `primarySummary` is the exact arithmetic mean of all twelve exact
paired differences. If any pair is unavailable, set it to null with an explicit
reason and retained planned denominator 12. An optional `completePairDescription`
must expose its denominator/seed IDs and is not the planned primary estimate.
No CI, bootstrap or significance label is planned. A later inferential procedure
would require prospective review before outcomes, not post-result selection.

## Secondary records and clear estimands

For all 48 checkpoints retain:

- The actual terminal NEXT clock/history, complete eventual cycle and transient,
  exact benefit/profit/price means, cycle length and support flags. Both final
  NEXT phases are4; only recurring phase4 executes a cap.
- Last-100k training means/counters and their integer source-to-destination count
  identities, separate from no-exploration frozen-policy results.
- Within-run 2M-minus-1M endpoint changes, which do not prove convergence.
- Actual cap-rule frequency, executed-price changes, largest-legal-action use,
  legal count/fraction/span and per-firm state/action support, with denominators.
- All-state cycle/coverage diagnostics, including unvisited/default Q actions,
  clearly secondary to the actual terminal path; phase0 is transient.
- Same-game cap-first floor and original fixed95 target comparisons where their
  premises apply, preserving exact signs and scope. An observed endpoint is not
  an independent proof of a floor.
- Any explicitly reset common-origin replay as a counterfactual diagnostic,
  never a replacement for actual continuation and never a fresh startup cap
  silently inserted into the primary endpoint.
- A 128-step truncated actual-terminal replay for graphics, distinct from exact
  full-cycle statistics and from the historical training trajectory.

Private profit, customer benefit, discounted Q training and undiscounted cycle
means keep distinct labels. Acceptance of legal actions is 100% by construction;
it is not a measure of strategic freedom, competence or protection.

4. Canonical dense uncompressed Q/count hashes and portable compressed artifact
   refs/byte ceilings, actor states, windows, cap count and clock/history.
5. Endpoint-specific independent from-zero replay with all required transcript,
   Q/count/actor/history/window and complete policy/graph comparisons, plus
   explicit unavailable/failed secondary and final checkpoint slots.
6. Final source/input/output drift checks and explicit full-replay/completeness
   flags scoped solely to this new 24-run study, never inferred from inventory
   acceptance alone.

Require all recorded round-transcript block hashes as well: each main round is
the protocol's canonical 120-byte record (one-based uint32 step; six uint8
phase/history/actions/flags; six reserved zeros; nine f64 epsilon/reward/old-Q/
next-max/new-Q values; eight post-draw actor uint32 words). Each fixed 10,000-round
block has a SHA256, ordered first/last indices and its run identity: 200 blocks
per complete run, 4,800 over 24 runs. Checkpoints bind their first 100/200 hashes.
Primary Buffer encoding and independent DataView encoding must agree, including
flags, padding, field order and block order. Incomplete tails are partial only.
The maximal artificial benchmark includes all encoding/hash overhead before
freeze; no outcome-dependent trace removal is allowed. Describe acceptance as
matching all recorded transcript-block hashes and checkpoint bits, not an
externally authenticated full raw log or a replay of any old training study.

Aggregate `fullTrainingReplay:true` AND `replayComplete:true` require all 24 runs
reaching 2M and all 48 checkpoint bit/graph comparisons passing: 48M replayed

false, with actual replayed/matched round counts and per-slot failures visible.
An honest complete 24-run/48-slot inventory may still pass its binding audit.

Each final endpoint requires its own independent full 2M replay from zero, all
200 transcript blocks, final Q/count/actor/history/clock/window and graph checks.

Outcome exports/graphics wait for authoritative inventory and endpoint-specific

Execution success, scientific endpoint availability, inventory acceptance and

Track their timers/resources separately. Checkpoint eligibility requires its

## Interpretation and presentation

The comparison conditions on one model, original masks/physical cap, a common
five-phase dense zero-initialized learner, twelve related seed identities and
a fixed two-million-round budget. It removes unequal clock-label count and
allocated Q capacity from this comparison. It does not establish that those
differences caused the old result, nor equalize legal support or visits.
The matched clock describes the scheduling rule, not a stationary fully observed
learning MDP: each seller omits the partner's evolving Q/internal state and faces
nonstationary behavior. Frozen joint-policy graphs and fixed-opponent MDP audits
have different scopes and do not supply a convergence theorem for joint learning.

Recurring enforcement affects training and frozen execution together; do not
describe its paired endpoint difference as a policy-formation-only causal effect.
Do not infer equilibrium, collusion intent/discovery, convergence, general market
protection or unrestricted learner capability. No new seller/model API calls or
human study occur. Codex assistance is development work.

Default graphical selections, if later authorized, must be chosen before outcome
inspection and stay fixed for unavailable/negative results. A simple proposed
default is seed 907001, final checkpoint, side-by-side actual continuations, with
both arms shown rather than selecting an attractive difference. Root must approve
that presentation choice before any new outcomes.
