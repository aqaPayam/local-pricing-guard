# Prospective learned-opponent derivatives and display

This is a source-only preparation contract for Goal 9. It supplements, and does
not replace, `LEARNED_OPPONENT_ANALYSIS_CONTRACT.md` and the primary protocol.
It authorizes no inherited checkpoint inspection, response solve or scientific
export. The V10 payload, report and navigation remain immutable.

## Population and exact quantities

Retain all 48 labels in seed907001..907012, controller startup-only/selected95,
seller A/B order. Preserve the raw primary record, its status, failure reason,
partial artifacts, numerical stopping information and source references. The
selected95 clock is the saved K4 controller. No K5 or mixed calendar is used.

Execution success and scientific availability are orthogonal. A retained budget
failure may contribute a scientific result only if its complete hashed f64
proposal, dyadic values, response policy, baseline/response paths and all-edge
certificate were fully received before termination and independently accepted.
No post-timeout salvage of a partial vector is allowed. Include every explicitly scientifically available result
and retain execution/status subgroup counts; do not discard a valid certificate
because its worker failed later. Incomplete partial artifacts are preserved but
do not supply a numerical gain. Final field names are `executionSucceeded` and
`scientificAvailable`, subject to the reviewed primary schema before freeze.

For each available record retain separately baseline and achieved response
infinite discounted private profit, signed raw achieved gain, all-edge upper
value, response optimality-gap bound, and possible best improvement interval
`[max(0, raw gain), upper-baseline]`. The zero fallback is a distinct feasible
baseline argument; it never replaces a negative response or missing solve.
Keep the exact requested gap and tight/loose classification. Relative raw gain
is null with a reason unless baseline is strictly positive. Preserve exact
baseline/response eventual-cycle customer-benefit means and their signed
difference as secondary quantities, not discounted welfare or a prefix bound.

Exact scalars retain the primary canonical reduced lowercase hexadecimal
numerator/positive denominator schema. Parse the minus sign separately, reject
negative zero, enforce token and one-million-bit limits before BigInt parsing,
and use bounded exact arithmetic. Decimal displays are outward conveniences;
signs, zero tests and comparisons come from exact scalars. No approximate
number is substituted for missing data or used to classify a gain.

Both baseline and response retain each firm's full-transient-plus-one-cycle
unweighted support counts and exact normalized discounted exposure to unvisited
states and never-tried chosen actions. These are distinct measures and are not
evidence about a partner that resumes learning. Preserve h/c and full path
artifact references; a visual preview does not replace those paths.

## Descriptive seed summaries

For each controller retain its 24 seller labels and exact positive/zero/negative
raw-gain counts, available/unavailable counts, range and median. Report all 12
seed-level arithmetic means across the two sellers, separately for each
controller. Report all 12 paired selected95-minus-startup differences between
those means. A missing seller makes its seed mean unavailable; a missing mean
makes that seed's paired difference unavailable, with the source identities
and reason retained. Do not impute zero or silently change denominators.

These are dependent counterfactuals from 12 whole-market seeds, not 48 independent
markets. No CI, bootstrap, p-value, market-population inference or convergence
claim is added. Positive achieved gain refutes unilateral optimality only
against that frozen opponent from that saved terminal state. A nonpositive
achieved gain with a loose upper bound does not establish stability.

The controller contrast compares separately trained policy pairs with different
clocks, legal experiences and phase capacities. Paired seed identities do not
make it a clock-only causal intervention, a cap-frequency causal effect or an
unrestricted capability control. Within one checkpoint, the achieved policy
gain is a fixed-opponent counterfactual from the same state.

This is a white-box response audit: the optimizer knows the complete frozen
partner lookup, legal masks and saved reward table. It does not model a
competitor discovering a response from limited observations. The game lets a
person practice local choices; it does not turn that practice into such an
information-limited learning experiment.

## Independent acceptance and provenance gate

The analysis API must be importable without IO. A supplied read-only IO adapter
supports artificial evidence fixtures and in-memory corruptions. Actual
derivatives wait for root's complete independent-audit acceptance. The local
gate additionally binds current source/input snapshots, manifest, aggregate,
index, completion, both journal/run-start receipts, every indexed record and
artifact, and the current independent auditor source.

The authoritative receipt must have passed=true, allPlannedLabelsRetained=true,
labels=48, and exact studyManifestHash/resultsSha256/indexSha256/
completionSha256/journalSha256/runStartSha256/auditorSourceHash bindings.
arithmeticBackendHash is null: the independent implementation uses its own
standard-library exact arithmetic. Primary execution success remains separate
from scientific acceptance: an independently accepted honest failure inventory
may have primaryExecutionPassed=false. Retain such slots as unavailable and
make no numerical claim for them. Frozen dependency/source mismatches block
export even if an older receipt says passed. Receipt/source field names will
be matched to the final reviewed primary and independent adapter before freeze.
If all 48 responses are unavailable, a passed inventory/binding audit is not
numerical response acceptance. Preserve the false
`actualScientificNegativeControlsAvailable` flag, zero certificate count and
null response results. Inventory corruption checks must not be relabelled
scientific certificate checks. Such an export is entirely locked for play.

Both provenance and display retain optimizerIterationsReplayed=false,
fullTrainingReplay=false and inheritedEconomicReenumeration=false. Checking
the saved f64 proposal, its exact dyadic quantization, final argmax and every
Bellman edge is not a replay of all optimizer sweeps or original training.
Accepted old finite payoff tables are reused through explicit hash bindings.
The accepted V10 receipt is bound; an available V10 ZIP is hash-checked without
requiring a nested old archive in portable evidence.

The web adapter must regenerate canonical analysis read-only and byte-match
the saved summary before export. Editing a derived summary alone must not
pass by leaving its input hash list unchanged. Synthetic tests must reject
malformed exact values, sign/gap/fallback confusion, missing paired units,
changed statuses/artifacts, false audit acceptance and stale bindings. Actual
corruption checks use in-memory read overrides and verify live inputs unchanged.

## Stable presentation contract

The planned payload is `{schemaVersion:1, meta, orderedSeeds, controllerOrder,
focalOrder, records:[48], controllerSummaries:[2], seedMeans:[24],
pairedDifferences:[12]}`. Records preserve primary scientific fields, with a
separate display object for exact-sign/outward-decimal conveniences and null
reasons. Meta includes authoritative audit, separate execution/scientific/status
success counts, exact
input/source bindings, resource scope and exclusions. A later game-data
extension is specified below and must not change science.

Default seed907001, startup-only, seller A was chosen before inspecting these
strategic outcomes. Keep it selected if zero, negative or unavailable. This is
a presentation decision, not a scientific factor or whole-project registration.
Any graphical continuation starts at the actual saved history/NEXT phase,
awards no old reward and introduces no fresh cap. Its finite executed discounted
total stays separate from audited infinite values. Label the opponent as a
deterministic saved policy. No language-model/chat/API seller calls, paid
inference or new training are introduced; Codex assistance is development work.

### Prospective manual-game extension

After complete independent acceptance, `webgameData` may add one unchanged
finite game, 24 deduplicated checkpoint cases (seed/controller), and 48 response
policy entries identified by scientific record id. Each checkpoint case has
its actual terminal/NEXT phase, original controller, all-state original greedy
A/B actions, exact decoded compact action counts, shared compact row offsets,
status/reason and source references. No Q float array or optimizer is shipped.

Export original greedy actions in explicit WORLD-state orientation: A maps
directly; B maps its saved own/rival row once. The offline independent audit
cross-checks the saved policy bytes against Q. Decoded u32 action counts remain
in explicitly labelled original OWN/rival row order, with ascending original
legal actions and reconstructed overflow counts. Browser support lookup for B
therefore swaps completed A/B history for those counts, but does not swap the
already-world-oriented greedy action array. Full row sums are exact state
visits. Preserve source encodings/hashes and verify dimensions/all-state
legality; these are deterministic representation changes, not new policies.

Each response entry preserves scientific availability and retained execution
status. It embeds the independently checked world-indexed response actions
only when scientifically available; otherwise actions are null with a reason.
Failure to validate a checkpoint locks that case rather than inventing a
partner. A checkpoint with both focal responses scientifically unavailable is
also locked: the new audit does not numerically reconstruct that checkpoint
in this situation. Old audit/hash provenance is not silently substituted for
a new validation result. Deduplication may not collapse any of the 48 scientific identities.
Artificial tests cover both orientations, cap-history dependence, overflow
counts, arbitrary off-path legal choices, null locks and source corruption.
Full accepted evidence includes every exact policy/path artifact; bounded game
previews and manual scores are separate from infinite certified values.
