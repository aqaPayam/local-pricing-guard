## Static anchors and interpretation limits

All pure Nash and exact joint-profit-maximizing profiles were independently
enumerated, with exact sums of separately decoded profits and every tie
retained. This particular table has one pure Nash profile, indices [6,6],
price approximately 1.472927 per firm, profit 22.292666 per firm and customer
benefit 71.509771. The unique joint-profit maximizer is [56,56], price
approximately 1.924981 per firm, joint profit 67.498092 and customer benefit
32.707805. Rounded figures here are descriptive; the receipt and primary
anchor artifact retain exact fractions.

The full-grid cycles remain well below the static joint-profit maximum.
They do not show a perfect cartel, profitable punishment, intent, equilibrium,
incentive stability or convergence. The guard also does not recover the static
Nash customer-benefit value in these runs. The supported conclusion is a
conditional total support-treatment effect, including changed learning
experiences and changed frozen evaluation support together. It is not a
learning-only effect or a universal prevention guarantee.

Equal dense allocation, initialization, streams and round budget do not equalize
learning difficulty. The wider action set changes visits per action and sample
efficiency. This is part of the registered intervention, not evidence of equal
competence. There are no unvisited source states or never-tried chosen actions
on any of the twelve final attained cycles; that narrower support observation
does not validate every off-path action or prove that values have converged.
Three of twelve cycle-benefit values differ between 1M and 2M. Unchanged values
in the other nine are not a convergence test.
