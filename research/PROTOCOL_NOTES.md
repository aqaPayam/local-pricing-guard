# Clarification after execution

The frozen protocol and the pre-execution gate review describe the immediate
best-response undercut near the elevated origin as roughly 0.24. The computed
prices are approximately 1.925 and 1.662, a cut of approximately 0.263. The final
report uses this computed value. The broad tolerance 0.30 still includes the cut;
the strict 0.005 and 0.05 values do not.

This is a correction to an approximate explanatory number, not a protocol,
source-code, parameter, outcome, or selection change. The frozen protocol,
manifest, source snapshots, and study outputs remain unchanged. All reported
probe payoffs were calculated from the actual prices, not the rough estimate.
