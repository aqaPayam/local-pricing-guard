## Scientific judgment

The common-five-phase control addresses the unequal public clock-label and
allocated-score-capacity objection to the old learner comparison. It has a
clear conditional estimand and preserves all twelve paired rows. It does not
identify the old gap's causes or isolate policy formation from continued
enforcement. Intentionally reused seeds and the absence of an unrestricted
capability arm remain limitations. This is an established-method control,
not a new Q algorithm or a claim that collusion is prevented.

Two independently authored implementations plus full replay improve execution
assurance for this new small study. Shared Node/V8/Math.exp/SHA backends remain
explicit. Matching recorded transcript-block hashes and checkpoint bits does
not externally authenticate every operation or retroactively replay the old
192-run experiment.
