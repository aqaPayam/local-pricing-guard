# Equal memory learning comparison

Repeated price limits increased eventual-cycle customer benefit in all twelve paired seeds of this controlled follow-up. The exact planned mean difference is `2420783571316325 / 2533274790395904`, approximately **+0.955595 benefit units**. This is a result about continued enforcement during training and frozen execution in one finite model. It does not establish learned collusion prevention, convergence or equilibrium.

## What the comparison changes

The earlier 192-run study gave different controllers different numbers of clock labels and Q rows. The follow-up gives both conditions the same five public phases, `0 → 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 1`, and the same dense allocated memory. Phase 0 happens once. Each seller has 1,191,640 binary64 Q cells and the same count-array allocation.

Both conditions use the unchanged original-tolerance 62-price game, zero initial scores, paired private random streams and two million simultaneous rounds. Startup applies the physical price limit only in phase 0; recurring applies it in phases 0 and 4. Other phases use the original near-best immediate-profit guard. A limit replaces the guard for that round. It is a real price-constrained market execution, with profit and customer benefit.

The twelve old seed namespaces are deliberately reused. Each arm retains its own actual final history and NEXT phase 4 when exploration and learning stop. Startup therefore continues with the guard while recurring continues with a limit. Neither receives a fresh initial cap. Equal allocation does not imply equal legal actions, experience or visits.

## Algorithm and checks

Each firm uses standard masked tabular Q-learning with its own deterministic expected profit, alpha 0.1 and binary64 gamma 0.95. Exploration declines from 1 to 0.01. Both current actions and both next-state bootstrap maxima are determined before either Q update. Legal original-index ties break toward the smallest index. The opponent has its own scores and random stream; no shared reward, seller communication or language-model player is used.

The prospective freeze binds 29 scientific sources, 182 inputs and all 24 ordered runs. Independently authored direct-scan code reexecutes all 48 million market rounds from zero, checking all 4,800 distinct 10K-round transcript hashes and all 48 complete Q/count checkpoints, actor states, histories, windows and greedy successor graphs. The authoritative receipt accepts all 24 final endpoints and both aggregate replay-completeness flags. There were no execution failures, omitted runs or favorable reruns.

This is separate implementation validation under the same V8 arithmetic and shared backend, using accepted saved economic tables. It is not a cross-runtime proof or externally authenticated raw per-round record. The older 192-run training trajectory remains unreplayed in full.

Primary execution took 301.532 seconds; independent replay and validation took 174.359 seconds. Approximate paired sampled working sets were 239,435,776 bytes and 256,614,400 bytes respectively. Sampling can miss peaks. Primary stored output before completion was 61,078,174 bytes; its raw-equivalent accounting was 1,428,563,993 bytes, including 1,372,769,280 canonical dense checkpoint bytes. No declared soft target was exceeded.

## Playing and interpreting the replay

The paired `/matched` view advances both accepted final worlds by one or twenty rounds. Previous prices begin as unearned memory. Undo and restart restore both histories and exact finite ledgers. The latest round, newly earned prefix average and complete eventual-cycle mean are distinct measurements. The fixed default remains seed 907001, chosen before the new matched outcomes. The older `/opponent` game separately lets a person choose one shop's prices against a saved learner.

The methods are established Q-learning, masked policies and exact functional-graph evaluation. The contribution here is a controlled architecture comparison and a fully replayed evidence chain alongside the model-specific protection analysis. Unrestricted learner capability, adaptation after unilateral changes, broader economic robustness and human comprehension remain open.
