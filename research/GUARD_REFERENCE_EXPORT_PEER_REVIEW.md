does not independently authenticate SHA labels, rederive anchor optimality or
replay training; the page says so. Static anchors, finite replay earnings,
training windows and eventual cycles have separate labels. No equilibrium,
collusion, convergence or prevention claim follows from their proximity.

This review establishes no native browser layout, accessibility/usability or
human comprehension result. Actual export regeneration, final input/output
closure and a bounded browser check still belong to delivery verification after
the authoritative numerical gate. No paid models, account-backed game players,
human-data claim or submission is introduced.
