careful execution evidence. It is not a demonstrated general collusion detector
or prevention method. Its main remaining weakness for judging is breadth and
field-level novelty, not a missing favorable numerical result. The guarded and
full-grid comparison materially improves the behavioral story, while the
reported individual-profit exception prevents a universal mutual-gain claim.

## Actionable clarity and reproduction observations

- Lead any cover note or walkthrough with the bounded finding: a local guard
  can help these learners while failing a stronger all-policy welfare promise.
  Then show the two separate contrasts: recurring versus startup guard, and
  full-grid after startup versus retained guard. Their opposite difference
  signs refer to different treatments; they are not conflicting estimates.
- The full-grid arm is a meaningful broader-choice reference at the stated
  budget, not fully unrestricted pricing from initialization or a positive
  control proving stable collusion. The 907009 Shop B exception, finite action
  board, unequal visits per option and changed training/execution support
  should remain visible. No new counterfactual is required to preserve that
  honest conclusion.
- Keep the fixed three-minute Play/Watch route as the entry point. It says who
  chooses prices, what one click earns and why the complete cycle differs from
  a watched prefix. Do not lead first-time visitors through raw hash ledgers
  or optional historical labs. Native developer observations support working
  controls and readable inspected regions; they do not establish human
  comprehension, exhaustive accessibility or operation on a physical phone.
- Numerical reproduction requires the full evidence archive and manifest
  runtime identity, not merely any Python 3.13 installation or the viewer's
  Node minimum. Preserve the pinned executable/backend/platform requirements
  next to the audit commands and explain a runtime rejection honestly. No
  cross-machine reproduction was performed in this review.
