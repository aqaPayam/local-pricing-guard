HOW MUCH ROOM WAS LEFT?

The calibration margin

A policy selected close to its original target may have little spare protection. The nearly 100% target already spends almost all the cap’s modeled benefit, so a tiny margin there is expected.

A failing policy at zero error has an empty safe set. That differs from a policy safe exactly at zero. These margins concern this one direction of model error.

What changed, and what this does not prove

Both products’ actual quality falls by d times the market’s taste scale. The outside option, number of buyers and taste scale stay fixed. External enforcement still uses its original demand model. The original absolute target stays fixed too.

The same upper-price path remains the worst permitted path because customer benefit still falls as either price rises. The checked endpoint therefore protects an entire declared uncertainty interval. It does not describe a probability of success, estimated demand or a real-market forecast.

Unknown enforcement, uncertain taste scale, other demand families, autonomous learning and sellers’ strategic stability are separate questions. No human comprehension or model-player experiment is claimed.
