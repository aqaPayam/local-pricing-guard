What does the guard do?

It permits prices whose modeled immediate profit is close to the best response to the rival’s previous price. It clips other proposals to the nearest permitted price. It cannot see the rival’s simultaneous choice.

This human game uses continuous prices and the original continuous guard. The tabular-learning study uses a finite price grid and a separately defined grid guard. A saved learned opponent is frozen: it does not learn from your play here. Starting at high prices is a challenge setup, not evidence that those prices emerged through learning.

Try the same quotes without protection, then with both shops guarded. At tolerance zero the guard determines the entire price; at tolerance eight it can permit the high-price path.
