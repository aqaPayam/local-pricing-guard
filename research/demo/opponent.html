A MANUAL SESSION IS NOT THE EXPERIMENT

Was the saved strategy worth following?

The research freezes one known lookup policy and audits all permitted choices of the other shop. A complete proposed response is evaluated exactly from the original training endpoint; an independently checked bound limits how much better any response could be in this finite model.

What exactly was checked?

This is a white-box audit: the complete saved rival policy, transition rule and numerical reward tables are known. It does not show that a learner or person could discover the response from observations. It is not a test of intent, mutual adaptation, every-state equilibrium or a real market.

Training used binary64 tabular Q learning. This follow-up uses exact discount 19/20 and exact rational values of saved numerical rewards; it does not retrain either shop or call a language model. The optimizer’s floating iterations were not replayed independently; its final policy, every allowed Bellman edge, complete paths and value bounds were.

All 48 retained comparisons

12 whole-market seeds × 2 original schedules × 2 focal shops. These are related comparisons, not 48 independent observations. No unavailable result is replaced by zero.
