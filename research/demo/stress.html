What this test can tell us

A learned policy shows what one training process produced. This audit asks what any coordinated permitted policy can do in the stated finite model. A constant price pair is a valid repeat pattern; a dramatic price cycle is not required.

A lower customer-benefit bound is not a per-move guarantee. It depends on the selected starting state, price grid, timing and known market formula. Neither profit nor a passing local check proves stable collusion or real-market safety.
