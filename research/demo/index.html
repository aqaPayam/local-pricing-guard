MATCHED COUNTERFACTUAL
What happens if A changes just one price?
Copy this exact market. Force A’s takeover quote to the best response to B’s last price, then resume the original rules. The change may be a cut, an increase, or zero. B never sees the current quote before choosing.
Solid: original path. Dashed: forced-quote path. A response is programmed; this test does not establish a learned equilibrium. An A policy may reset on its own changed price even if B never responds.
