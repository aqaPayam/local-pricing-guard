WHAT THE RESULTS SUPPORT

A useful failure case, with clear limits.

Information channel matters

Deleting a note interrupts a note-following script. A rule that copies numeric prices can continue without that note. Both outcomes follow our disclosed assumptions.

Template value is unproven

The structured note adds no pricing benefit beyond note deletion here. The 24 safety-target passes compare against keeping the old strategy, not against facts only.

Do not add an irrelevant learner

A tabular learner that ignores text would not test semantic handover. A future language study needs matched notes, independent market runs and separately authorized inference.

Reproducible evidence

All original price paths, one-shot deviations, source snapshots and completion hashes are saved locally. The independent audit recomputed 112,320 settled rounds. Earlier exploratory LLM pilots are excluded from this study.

No new Codex/ChatGPT game-player requests or paid compute were used. Development and review agents are separate from game players.
