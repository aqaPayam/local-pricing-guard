THE QUESTION IN PLAIN LANGUAGE

Can a light touch
protect an average?

The guard checks each shop’s immediate profit against the other shop’s previous price. That local check is different from a promise about customer benefit over many rounds.

A cap really limits both executed prices for one round. We study what happens when the guard remains, when it is removed after that first cap, and when the same cap returns every few rounds.
