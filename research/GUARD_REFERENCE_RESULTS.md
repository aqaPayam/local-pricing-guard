# What changes when the guard is removed?

Status: accepted numerical evidence. The new report, native replay QA and delivery are reviewed separately.

The guard **helps these learners**: customer benefit is lower in all twelve full-grid continuations. The registered reference-minus-guard exact-cycle mean is **-12.732973 units**. This is consistent with the earlier mathematical finding that a helpful local guard still does not guarantee a stronger welfare target for every allowed policy.

Both versions use the same initial real cap, original 62-price payoff table, five clock labels, dense memory, zero initialization, paired random streams and two-million-round budget. After the first cap, the old version retains its immediate-profit guard; the new version allows the full original price grid. The guard changes training and frozen execution together.

## Secondary checks and important exceptions

The separately registered final 100K exploratory-training windows also favor the guard in all twelve pairs: exactly computed mean reference-minus-guard benefit is approximately -13.312819. Training windows, deterministic eventual cycles and rounds watched in the browser are different endpoints.

Final cycle customer benefit changes from 1M to 2M in 3/12 new runs. The other nine matching checkpoints do not prove convergence. Every new final-cycle state and chosen greedy action had training support; that does not establish competence, equilibrium or discoverability.

Exact enumeration retains one pure Nash pair `[6,6]` and one joint-profit maximum `[56,56]` on the unchanged simultaneous table. All ties would be retained; joint profit is added as exact rational weights. These are static stage-game anchors, not learned-policy equilibrium certificates.

23/24 full-grid individual cycle profits exceed the static Nash profit. The exception is seed 907009 Shop B: about 19.1434 versus 22.2927. **Both shops do not always gain.** The full-grid reference is below the joint-profit maximum. High prices, aggregate profit and customer harm alone do not establish collusion, punishment or intent.

Equal memory and rounds do not equalize legal action counts, visits per action or learning difficulty. This is one finite calibration and a staged follow-up reusing twelve old seed namespaces. It is not a newly independent market population, continuous unrestricted pricing, or proof about other training budgets.
