# Prospective manual continuation game

Design only. No new graphical player or outcome export before complete
independent acceptance of the frozen48-label study. Preserve V10 assets.

## Plain question

Two local programs have learned prices. Freeze one program. Can the other
shop earn more by changing its choices, while keeping the same guard and cap?
A customer-protection rule and a shop's incentive to follow its old strategy
are separate questions. The study measures that distinction; a favorable
manual session is not research evidence.

## What a person controls

The person runs one clearly named shop, A or B. The other shop follows its
actual saved deterministic lookup policy, with no learning, chat or model API.
Both choices use the previous completed price pair and current original
clock. The rival does not observe the person's current choice before choosing.
Every original permitted price remains available. No clipping or extra action.

Start at the actual saved final training history and NEXT clock phase. The
already executed prices are labelled unearned memory. No new cap is invented:
startup-only continues under the guard forever, while the savedK4 clock can
naturally have a cap next. Only new executed rounds earn local practice scores.

Default is seed907001, startup-only, shopA, chosen before new response outcomes.
Retain that default if its result is zero or unavailable. A controller/seed/role
change resets the local practice session and discloses why. Advanced seed
choices show all12 seeds; controls do not imply48 independent samples.

## A small, readable play loop

1. Show the current cap/guard rule and which previous rival price determines
   the person's allowed choices. Show both shops and their last actual local
   execution, or dashes before the first one.
2. Choose one permitted quote. Helpers fill the lowest, highest or certified
   proposed-policy quote; filling is not execution. The certified choice is
   disabled with a reason if no valid response certificate is available.
3. Execute one simultaneous pair. Reveal the frozen rival's selected quote,
   modeled purchases, both expected profits and customer benefit.
4. Update earned discounted profit and the same-length baseline reference.
   Undo and restart must restore clock, history and all exact ledgers together.

The baseline reference is a separate counterfactual from the same stored start,
with both original policies. It follows its own prices, never the player's
modified history. Its next turn shares the original public clock, not a fake
reset. A finite-prefix profit difference may be negative even when the full
certified response has a positive infinite discounted gain.

## Distinct score meanings

- Earned profit so far: executed expected private profits, discounted by19/20
  each round, with no training memory or future profit added.
- Baseline reference so far: the same number of counterfactual rounds under
  both original saved policies, explicitly not the player's earned profit.
- Study evidence: exact infinite baseline/response values from the original
  stored state, attained gain and remaining possible improvement bound.
  These describe following the entire proposed policy, not arbitrary manual
  choices or money already earned.
- Customer benefit: observed local round/running values and separate complete
  study cycle means. Cycle means, discounted profit and every-prefix welfare
  protection must not share one label or score.

## Graphics and evidence

Use the existing visual language: orange shopA, dashed-blue shopB, readable
price cards, scaled expected-purchase baskets, cap markers and a short memory
arrow diagram. Stack shop cards on narrow screens; keep numerical labels
readable and use contained horizontal scrolling for wide charts/tables.
Plot executed prices and cumulative discounted profit versus the explicitly
counterfactual baseline, without awarding future rewards. Distinguish colors
with labels/line styles as well.

A separate evidence view may reveal stored baseline/response trajectories one
step at a time. Label previews as stored evidence, not new executions or an
estimator of the exact infinite value. If a current opponent state/action was
unvisited/untried during training, describe that exact fact, not incompetence
or a prediction of what retraining would do. Show whole-path discounted
unsupported exposure separately from simple unique-state counts.

All48 evidence rows and their failure/loose-gap reasons remain accessible.
Unknown/invalid data locks play rather than silently falling back to a
different game or invented opponent. Hash strings in the page are provenance
labels; the offline independent auditor supplies numerical acceptance.

## QA and claims

Before delivery test actual-terminal startup/K4 clocks, sellerB transpose,
simultaneity, off-path policy lookup, every allowed price, exact discounting,
baseline's separate history, cooperative/adverse manual choices, undo/reset,
unavailable data and desktop/390/320 layouts. Use artificial full-state games
and the entire accepted selection matrix where relevant. Native browser checks
are developer QA, not a human study or accessibility/device certification.
The static local server receives no game-player API calls or uploads.
