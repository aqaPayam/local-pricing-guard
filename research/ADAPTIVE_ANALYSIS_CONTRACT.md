# Prospective Goal 7 descriptive analysis contract

This contract proposes analysis only. It authorizes no training, primary case
execution or export before the root's distinct-goal/source-review gates. Final
identities and endpoint definitions follow the prospectively reviewed primary
protocol. Earlier frozen evidence remains unchanged.

## Primary comparison and statistical unit

The declared design retains 192 runs: two inherited mask conditions, four
controllers, twelve fresh paired whole-market seeds and two Q initializations.
The two games share one economic calibration; they are mask conditions, not two
independent economies. Q initialization and checkpoint differences do not create
independent seed replicates.

There are exactly four primary strata: game crossed with Q initialization. In
each, retain twelve paired seed rows with outcome

    final terminal-history frozen-greedy-cycle customer benefit:
    closed-95%-period controller minus startup-only controller.

Use each run's actual saved terminal public history AND controller clock. The
startup-only arm receives no new cap at evaluation. These are deterministic
frozen-policy long-run cycle averages after finite training, not proof that the
training process converged. All zero/negative differences remain in the table.
If any required pair is unavailable, that stratum's planned twelve-pair estimate
and interval remain unavailable; do not silently select complete pairs or seeds.

For review, the bootstrap implementation is fixed as follows: Mulberry32 with
unsigned initial state 20261026 generates one 5120-by-12 array of indices by
floor(12*u). Twelve ordered, prospectively declared seed IDs are resampled with
replacement. Use the SAME draw for all controller arms and all four strata,
preserving the matched whole-seed structure. Each draw averages the twelve
selected paired differences. The estimate is their original twelve-pair mean.
Report marginal percentile endpoints at .025/.975 using linear/type7 interpolation
on sorted 5120 means (h=(5120-1)*p). Save the index matrix and its SHA256. These
are marginal 95% intervals, without familywise/simultaneous coverage claims.
Their floating endpoints are approximate descriptive seed-variation summaries
conditional on this learner, masks, initialization and budget. They do not
measure market/model uncertainty; exact payoff and floor signs remain separate.
Rounds, firms, phases, checkpoints and graph attractors are never bootstrap units.

## Required descriptive views

The gated summary will retain `runs[192]`, each original identity, source hashes,
status/failure and the two checkpoint records. Per checkpoint retain:

- terminal-history start and clock, deterministic reachable tail/cycle identity,
  cycle consumer surplus, both profits and prices, length and visited-state/
  visited-action flags;
- all-state frozen-policy graph summaries, including unvisited-state flags,
  explicitly secondary to the actual terminal-history reachable cycle, with
  startup-cap reachable states distinguished from arbitrary states outside its
  forward-invariant region;
- common-fresh-cap replay, with reset history/clock stated and no replacement of
  the primary continuation endpoint;
- the first 128 actual-terminal frozen-policy executions as a clearly truncated
  graphical preview, separate from exact full-cycle metrics; browser payloads
  contain the two finite game tables and summaries, not Q or full policy arrays;
- last-100k training measurements, both private profits, prices and customer
  benefit, exploration and actual cap activation frequency;
- legal-mask action counts/spans/fractions and maximum-allowed-action use, with
  conditioning and denominator definitions preserved;
- separately certified correct same-game floors and original fixed 95% target
  gaps, retaining exact rational signs and approximate display values separately.

Top-level grouped `rows[2]` retain all controller/init/seed identities. Flat
`primaryPairs[48]` and `primaryContrasts[4]` retain all twelve pairs per stratum,
bootstrap provenance and missingness. `secondaryRows` preserve controller, Q
initialization, checkpoint, evaluation origin and seed, preventing accidental
pooling. Training and frozen-policy endpoints keep distinct names and scopes.

## Evidence gate and interpretation

Before generating results, require passed current independent audit(s) binding
manifest, aggregate results/index, live auditor/backend identities, every run and
checkpoint, complete start/finish journals and final frozen-dependency rechecks.
Verify trace/checkpoint inventory hashes without claiming complete training
replay unless the independent audit actually recomputes all training updates.
Keep failed/unfinished runs and resource overruns; never adapt the matrix or
training horizon after seeing outcomes.

Own-profit independent Q learning is observed behavior, not a prescribed
upper-price script. It still does not establish collusion or equilibrium. The
Goal 6 state-independent reset-block unilateral optimum is not transferable to
learned cap actions that depend on completed history. There is no unrestricted
learner-capability reference in this four-controller matrix, so low prices cannot
be attributed to prevention of emergent collusion. All findings remain specific
to these masks, learner/update/exploration settings, initialization and finite
budget. The independently proved all-policy welfare floors are distinct from
the empirical behavior and uncertainty intervals.
