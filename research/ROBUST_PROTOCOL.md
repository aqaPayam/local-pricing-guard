## Fixed design

All buyers100 outsideQuality0. These are calibration sensitivities, not broad real-market coverage. Lower quality strengthens the outside option. Changing mu affects own/cross/outside substitution jointly.

Compute each economy's continuous symmetric Nash and symmetric joint-profit stationary optimum by bracketed root finding; verify first-order conditions numerically. Enumerate every finite-grid pure Nash equilibrium and all joint-profit pairs. Use each economy's own price span: Nash minus10% of Nash-to-joint gap to joint plus10%.
