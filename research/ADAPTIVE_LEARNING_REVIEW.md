## Question and scope

Two independently adapting own-profit Q tables add behavior that was not set
by the adverse upper-price script. Comparing the same mask and physical cap
under different cap timings is meaningful. It remains conditional on these
finite numerical games, two initializations, this learner and finite training
budget. There is no unrestricted learner-capability reference; one cannot claim
that the controller prevents learned collusion. The two proposed games are
mask conditions in the same economic calibration, not independent economies.

The primary evaluation must freeze the final Q pair and continue from its
actual terminal completed-price history and clock. A startup-only arm receives
no fresh cap. The separately labeled common-history fresh-cap diagnostic is
a counterfactual restart, not the observed training continuation. A deterministic
frozen policy's eventual cycle does not establish that training converged.

Both cap-state previous-price coordinates remain observed. A learned opponent
can condition its cap quote on that history; hence the fixed-script Goal6
state-independent next-block constant and last-guard one-shot argument cannot
be transferred. A complete joint functional-graph audit is appropriate without
asserting unilateral best response, equilibrium or strategic intent.

The reward must be labeled deterministic saved expected profit; no sampled
buyers or reward noise is specified. Source/destination marginal counts alone
cannot reconstruct maximum-permitted-action use, so that reported metric needs
its own exact counters or a joint transition inventory. Root also requested
clone-and-discard diagnostic probes, including at the final 2M checkpoint: they
must not extend the training horizon or mutate terminal Q/counts/PRNG/window

All-state frozen cycles must be distinguished from terminal-reachable and
policy-cap-reachable cycles. In particular arbitrary old guard-state cycles
in startup-only need not obey the cap-start invariant floor. The actual
exploratory training history can also reach a state that is not reachable
under the final deterministic greedy profile from any cap state. Neither
classification should erase the actual terminal continuation.
