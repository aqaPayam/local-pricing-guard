# Prospective interpretation contract: learned-opponent incentives

Draft for the next separately frozen stage. Preparing this document does not
authorize inherited-market computation or change the accepted V10 evidence.
Root, primary developer and independent referee must agree the final protocol,
source, synthetic checks and input inventory before any new case executes.

## Question and unit

Continue each saved market from its actual final 2M terminal history and next
clock phase. Can one seller increase its discounted private profit by replacing
its policy while its original learned opponent remains frozen? The original
local guard, physical cap, legal price set and controller remain unchanged.
The primary matrix has 12 whole-market seeds, two controllers and both focal
sellers: 48 paired counterfactual audits in one original-tolerance, zero-score
initialization stratum of one economic calibration. There is no new training.

This targeted matrix is selected after the old welfare results are known but
before calculating its strategic-response outcomes. It is a prospective
numerical follow-up, not a preregistration before the entire research project.
Do not expand the matrix or adjust its solver budget after seeing gains.

## Primary quantities

Retain every planned label, including invalid inputs, interrupted solvers,
budget failures and loose certificates. For each successfully checked label,
show separately:

1. Exact baseline discounted value under both original greedy policies.
2. Exact achieved value of the proposed legal stationary response against the
   same frozen opponent, from the same actual terminal state.
3. Raw achieved gain, including negative and exactly zero gains.
4. The independently certified all-state/action Bellman upper value and the
   response's nonnegative remaining optimality-gap upper bound.
5. An interval for the best possible unilateral improvement over baseline:
   lower endpoint max(0, achieved gain), upper endpoint U minus baseline.
   The explicit baseline-as-legal-choice argument justifies the zero lower
   bound; it must not hide a failed or negative proposed response.
6. Relative achieved gain only when the exact baseline is strictly positive;
   otherwise retain a null value and its reason.

Every sign is exact for the saved numerical rewards and mathematical discount
19/20. Approximate displayed decimals cannot decide positivity or optimality.
The audit discount is explicitly distinguished from binary64 training gamma.
The exact all-edge gap target is 1e-6 absolute discounted-profit units. A solve
that misses it remains a certified feasible response if its certificate is
valid, with an explicit loose-gap status; it is not an exact optimum.

Execution success and scientific availability are separate fields. A hard
timeout remains a budget failure, and other process failures keep their own
typed statuses. A case can nevertheless be scientifically
available only if the complete bound final-f64/dyadic/policy/path/all-edge
certificate artifact set and report were fully received before termination
and pass independent checking. A partial vector does not authorize a later
primary salvage calculation or invented completed certificate. Incomplete
cases retain null scientific quantities with reasons. Include every accepted
scientifically available case in descriptive gain tables, while separately
reporting execution successes, budget failures and loose-gap statuses.

## Secondary welfare link

For every successful label, compute the exact eventual-cycle customer-benefit
mean under the baseline and the response, and their signed difference. Use
the independently replayed full joint path, its transient/cycle split and the
unchanged saved consumer-surplus weights. These are cycle means, separate
from discounted profit values and every-prefix safety guarantees. Do not use
them to choose the response or break any optimization tie. Failures retain
null values with explicit reasons. This adds no cases or solver objective and
does not establish a learning effect.

## Summaries and comparisons

Show all 48 labels in stable seed/controller/seller order. Each controller's
24 seller outcomes are dependent within its 12 market seeds. Report counts of
strictly positive, exactly zero and negative raw gains, range and median; these
are descriptive finite-population quantities, not independent sample tests.
Also show all 12 seed-level arithmetic means across its two sellers for each
controller, available only when both seller values are independently accepted;
otherwise retain a null and its missing-label reason. If comparing controllers,
retain the 12 paired differences between those seed-level achieved-gain means,
with null reasons unless both controller means are available. Report the
available count for every descriptive summary. No additional bootstrap, p-value or
population-confidence claim is planned for this exact policy audit.

The controller contrast compares separately trained saved policy pairs with
different clocks, legal action experiences and phase-table capacities. It is
not a clock-only intervention on one unchanged learned pair, or an isolated
causal estimate of cap frequency. Its seed pairing supplies a descriptive
within-design contrast, not an unrestricted capability control.

Show numerical upper-gap size alongside the measured gain. A negative or zero
proposed gain with a loose upper bound does not establish stability. Positive
achieved gain rejects unilateral optimality against that particular frozen
policy, even when the solver's remaining gap is loose. A very small certified
upper improvement bound supports only corresponding approximate optimality
from the specified terminal state, not an equilibrium at every state.

## Coverage, resources and exclusions

For baseline and response trajectories, retain full-state transient/cycle
lengths and state/action training-coverage flags for both firms. Retain counts
over the full transient plus one cycle and, separately, exact normalized
discounted exposure `(1-gamma) * sum_t gamma^t * indicator_t` for unvisited
states and never-tried chosen actions. Compute the infinite indicator sequence
with its full transient and repeated cycle, and verify each exposure lies in
[0,1]. This distinguishes early unsupported behavior from distant states that
carry little discounted weight. Such dependence describes the frozen lookup policy;
it does not predict the opponent's response if it resumed learning.

Save the iteration count, stopping reason and elapsed/resource records for
every label; intermediate value vectors need not be retained.
Approximate iteration is proposal generation; an unchanged floating vector is
not an exact mathematical convergence proof. Preserve overruns and unresolved
labels. Do not repair a scientific outcome by rerunning a seed, shortening a
cycle, changing the discount or substituting a baseline as an optimized result.

The complete original training trajectory remains not independently replayed.
This follow-up validates saved policy-response certificates, not that missing
trajectory. No API/model/chat players, paid inference, new human study,
unrestricted capability arm, collusive-intent test or mutual adaptation is
introduced.

The optimizer is a full-information audit with the saved rival policy, legal
supports and payoff table available. It does not show that a deployed rival
could discover the response from a few observations or without that access.

Frozen opponents are clearly labelled deterministic saved policies.

## Prior work and scope

Full-policy best responses to frozen learned pricing partners already appear
in Lopes de Moraes and De Losso, *Algorithmic Collusion Without Deterrence*,
arXiv2610.21867v2, Sections3.2(iv) and4.5. Root re-read the primary HTML source
on26Oct2026: <https://arxiv.org/html/2610.21867v2>.
This follow-up applies that question to the guarded, phase-aware saved games
and adds independently checkable numerical bounds. It makes no claim to invent
dynamic programming, frozen-opponent audits or the distinction between a
one-step deviation and a full response policy. The prior paper's reported
effect sizes are not expected results, acceptance thresholds or evidence for
our market.
